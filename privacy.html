Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Bride Vibes (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides the Bride Vibes mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Bride Vibes works on your device and does not require you to create an account. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location to use the app.

Depending on how you use the app, the following may apply:

  • On-device preferences: settings such as whether you have completed onboarding may be stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases: if you unlock premium content, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple may provide purchase information necessary to verify, unlock, or restore your purchase.

How We Use Information

Any information processed in connection with the app is used only to:

  • Provide the Bride Vibes experience
  • Unlock or restore in-app purchases

Third-Party Services

Purchases and App Store services are provided by Apple. Apple’s handling of information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you delete the app. If you contact us for support, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you.

Children’s Privacy

Bride Vibes is intended for adults planning bachelorette celebrations and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can stop using the app at any time and delete it from your device. For purchase questions, you can also review your purchase history through your App Store purchase settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
